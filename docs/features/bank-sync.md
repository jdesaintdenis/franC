# Bank sync

Connect your bank once, then one tap on **Sync now** brings its new booked transactions into franC, through the same pipeline as a CSV: merchant names, rules, guesses, Review, transfers and recurring payments. The connection goes through **Enable Banking**, a licensed provider: you log in on your bank's own page, and franC never sees your bank password. A daily job syncs by itself, franC syncs again when you open it, and each connection says how it is doing (Slice 49). A CSV import and the sync can feed the same account without counting a purchase twice (see **Duplicates** below).

## Using it

- **Accounts > Bank sync > Manage** (`/accounts/bank`). The row and the page only exist when the server has the Enable Banking variables set **and** your email is in `BANK_SYNC_EMAILS` (see `docs/deployment.md`); for anyone else the page and both routes are a 404. Enable Banking's free mode reads the application owner's linked accounts, so other people allowed to sign up on the same deployment never get the feature.
- **Bank** (title of the page since Slice 62). With no connection it shows a **Link your bank** card (your transactions arrive on their own every morning; you sign in on the bank's own page, franC never sees the password; read-only, 180 days at most, through Enable Banking), a **Without sync** row to **Import a CSV**, and a **Choose my bank** button at the bottom of the screen. That button opens **Your bank**: the French banks Enable Banking offers, Caisse d'Épargne ones first, with a search field (accents and case ignored). Tapping a bank goes to its login page.
- After you approve at the bank, you come back to the last step: for each bank account ("Compte de chèques ••1234") a button shows which franC account it **feeds**; it opens a sheet with your franC accounts, **New account "…"** or **Don't import**. Pre-selected: the franC account with the same name; else, if you have exactly one free account, that one for the first bank account; else a new account named after the bank's. Below, **one** date for the whole connection, **Bring transactions from** (Slice 62): the earliest of the accounts' defaults (the day after each franC account's newest transaction), so the sync picks up where your CSVs stopped and overlap costs nothing since sync and CSV dedupe against each other; 90 days back at most (today − 89, also the default for an empty or new account), today at the latest. Editing the date stops the suggestion from following later choices. The date shows until the first sync into that account. **Save and sync** applies everything at once, shows "Bank connected" and starts the first sync; **Disconnect** is a text button under it.
- Once linked, the card shows the bank with a **⋯** menu (**Change links**, **Disconnect bank**, which asks first), the connection's status line, and each franC account with its bank account (••1234), its sync status (**Not synced yet · from 26 Sept**, then **Synced 02/10/2026 10:42 · 12 new**) and its balance ("Not imported" when skipped), then "Connection valid until 31 Mar" and a full-width **Sync now** that syncs every imported account of that connection. **Change links** reopens the same form (button **Save**, **Cancel** under it).
- **Sync now** (also on the **Import** page, in a "Connected to <bank>" card above the drop zone when the chosen account is linked): the button reads "Syncing…" and is disabled meanwhile; then **12 new transactions** with **Review now**, or **Nothing new**. Rows the bank sent that franC can't read are counted ("1 transaction from the bank couldn't be read"), never dropped silently. A failure is one sentence: the bank refused access (the consent may have ended), isn't answering, sent something unexpected, or no longer knows the account, or the network failed.
- Synced transactions wait in **Review**, categorized by your rules or guessed, with a short label like a CSV row's.
- **Import history** lists every sync run, even one that brought nothing, as **Synced from <bank>** with its counts. Sync runs have **no Undo** (undone rows would come back on the next sync); to get rid of a synced row for good, [ignore it](transactions.md#ignoring-a-transaction).
- **Possible duplicates**: when a synced row could be one of several CSV rows, it is inserted and flagged **Possible duplicate** in [Review](review-queue.md#possible-duplicates), where you choose **Same transaction** or **Keep both**. The result of Sync now counts it among the new transactions until you decide.
- The transaction sheet says where a row came from: **From your bank** or **From CSV, 12 Sept** ([Transactions](transactions.md)).
- **Disconnect** never deletes a transaction, an account or any history. It ends the consent at Enable Banking and forgets the connection and its pairings, so connecting again asks for them again. Each account keeps its **last bank balance**, which becomes its editable starting balance again (the dialog says so).
- **The balance comes from the bank**: each Sync now also reads the account's booked balance. On a connected account the balance is the bank's, read-only, with "From Caisse d'Épargne, 2 Oct 07:00"; Home's Needs attention says when franC's transactions don't add up to it ([Bank accounts and balance](accounts-and-balance.md#gap-check-connected-accounts)).
- **Accounts** shows "Linked to <bank>" under each account a bank account feeds.
- If something goes wrong (you cancel at the bank, the attempt expired, Enable Banking refuses the credentials or doesn't answer), the page says so in one sentence and nothing is connected.

## Daily sync and connection health (Slice 49)

- **Daily job** (`GET /api/cron/daily`, Vercel Cron 07:00 UTC, `CRON_SECRET`, `maxDuration` 60): first the bank sync (`runDailySync`, `src/lib/bank-sync/daily.ts`, injected deps): every imported link of every `active` connection whose `valid_until` is in the future and whose user passes `BANK_SYNC_EMAILS`, one after another, through `syncAccount` **without PSU headers** (so it uses one of the bank's unattended fetches a day; Sync now isn't counted). Each attempt is recorded (`recordAttempt`), then transfers and recurring are refreshed for each user that got rows. Skipped when Enable Banking isn't configured. Then the [import reminder](import-reminder.md) push and the bank health push, both only when VAPID is configured. The log line holds counts only (`bank-sync cron: links=3 synced=3 inserted=12 failed=0 rateLimited=0`).
- **Sync on open**: `syncOnOpenAction()` (`sync-action.ts`) syncs the user's imported links whose last sync is **over 2 hours old** (`syncDue`, `SYNC_ON_OPEN_MS`); a link **never synced** is skipped (it waits for its first Sync now, so the start date stays changeable and the first, larger import is your call; the daily job still takes it), with PSU headers like Sync now. It re-checks the 2 hours on the server, so several tabs or reloads make one sync; it returns `{ ran: false }` or `{ ran: true, inserted }` and shows nothing on failure (the status does). `hasConnectedAccount(db, userId)` says whether the client trigger is needed.
- **Attempts** (`recordAttempt`, `src/db/queries/bank-sync.ts`): after every attempt, from the cron and from Sync now alike, the link gets `last_attempt_at`, `last_error` (null after a success) and `failing_since` (start of the current run of failures: set on the first failure, kept while it lasts, cleared by a success; a **rate limit never sets or clears it**).
- **Rate limit**: a 429 whose JSON body says `ASPSP_RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` is the error `rate_limited` ("Your bank's limit for today is reached. franC will try again tomorrow morning."); any other 429 stays `bank_unavailable`. The body is read with Zod and never logged.
- **Health** (`connectionHealth`, pure, `src/lib/bank-sync/health.ts`; `listBankConnections` returns it as `health`): `reconnect` (`expiresOn`, `expired`) when the consent ends in **14 days or less**, has ended, or an imported link's last error is `unauthorized`; else `failing` (`since`) when an imported link has `failing_since`; else `rateLimited`; else `ok` (`syncedAt` = the oldest `last_synced_at` of its imported links) or `never`.
- **Needs attention** on Home: "Reconnect your bank (expires 28 Mar / expired 28 Mar)" and "Sync failing since 3 Oct" (from the first failure), right after a balance below its alert (`bankReconnect`, `bankFailing` in `attention.ts`).
- **Health push** (`runBankHealthPush`, `src/lib/push/run-reminders.ts`): to allowed users with a subscribed device, when a connection needs a **reconnect**, or has been failing **since before this run** (the second failed morning in a row), and none was sent in the last 7 days (`users.bank_health_notified_on`). Generic localized text (`common.bankHealthPush`), no bank or account names; tapping opens `/accounts/bank`.
- **Reconnect**: `GET /api/bank-sync/connect?aspsp=…&country=FR&reconnect=<connectionId>`. The connection must be the user's (else `not_found`); the pending row stores `replaces_connection_id`. On the callback, in **one DB transaction**, each new bank account is paired with an old link (`matchReconnectedAccounts`: same uid, else same `identification_hash`, else same IBAN end and name) and takes over its franC account, start date, last sync and link time; the old connection is deleted (only the links cascade: transactions, matches and balance checkpoints belong to franC accounts and stay). If every old imported link was found, any extra new account is saved as not imported; otherwise the unmatched new accounts stay unlinked and the link form opens with the found ones pre-selected. The old session is ended at Enable Banking after the commit, best effort. The callback redirects with `?reconnected=1` (toast "Bank reconnected"). Cancelling at the bank leaves the old connection as it was.
- Columns: `bank_account_links.last_attempt_at`, `last_error`, `failing_since`, `identification_hash` (Enable Banking's account fingerprint, stored at connect and reconnect); `bank_connections.replaces_connection_id`; `users.bank_health_notified_on`. Migration `0030_bank_sync_health`.

## How it works

- **Enable Banking client** (`src/lib/bank-sync/client.ts`): every call carries a fresh RS256 JWT signed with the application's private key (`kid` = application id, `iss` `enablebanking.com`, `aud` `api.enablebanking.com`, valid 1 hour), made with `node:crypto` (no dependency). Responses are checked with Zod (unknown fields allowed). Failures become a `BankSyncError` code (`unauthorized`, `not_found`, `rate_limited`, `bank_unavailable`, `invalid_response`, `network`); nothing from the response body is logged. 15 s timeout per call.
- **Configuration** (`config.ts`): `ENABLE_BANKING_APP_ID`, `ENABLE_BANKING_PRIVATE_KEY` (the .pem content as is, with `\n` instead of line breaks, or base64 of the file) and optional `ENABLE_BANKING_API_ORIGIN` (tests point it at the fake server). Missing or invalid values hide the feature. `BANK_SYNC_EMAILS` (comma-separated, case-insensitive, unset = nobody) picks who gets it (`isBankSyncAllowedFor`).
- **Connect** (`GET /api/bank-sync/connect?aspsp=…&country=FR`): checks the bank is in Enable Banking's list, deletes this user's pending attempts older than 1 hour, stores a `pending` connection with a random 24-byte `auth_state`, asks Enable Banking for the bank's login URL (`POST /auth`, `psu_type` personal) and redirects there. It is a link to a route, not a form, because the CSP's `form-action 'self'` would block a form whose response leaves the site.
- **Consent length**: 180 days, or less when the bank's `maximum_consent_validity` is shorter. The bank's own `valid_until` from the session wins when present.
- **Callback** (`GET /api/bank-sync/callback?code&state`, or `?error`): the `state` must match a pending connection **of the signed-in user**, which blocks a forged or replayed callback. Then `POST /sessions` turns the code into a session; the connection becomes `active` with its session id, `valid_until`, and one `bank_account_links` row per approved bank account. On cancel or failure the pending row is deleted and the page shows the error code's sentence.
- **Links**: saving runs in one transaction (new accounts are created there, and rolled back if another choice fails). A franC account can be fed by only one bank account (partial unique index); other connections' accounts are disabled in the select. Deleting a franC account keeps the bank account row and clears its account.
- **Disconnect**: `DELETE /sessions/{id}` at Enable Banking (best effort; a failure there still disconnects in franC), then, in one DB transaction, each linked account's latest bank balance checkpoint is copied to its manual one (`promoteLatestBankCheckpoints`) and the connection and its links are deleted. Transactions, imports and accounts aren't touched; the bank checkpoints stay as history.
- **Balance** (`client.getBalances`, `balance.ts` `pickBookedBalance`): `GET /accounts/{uid}/balances`, validated loosely like the other schemas. `pickBookedBalance` takes only **booked** types, in the order **CLBD** (closing booked) > **ITBD** (interim booked) > **OTHR**; `XPCD` and `ITAV` include pending card payments, which franC doesn't import, so they would never add up. EUR only; the amount goes through the cents parser (no floats). Its day is `reference_date`, else the Paris day of `last_change_date_time`, else the Paris day of the fetch. `syncAccount` reads it **after** the transactions and saves it as the day's bank checkpoint in the same commit (a second sync the same day replaces the amount and keeps an accepted gap). If only the balance call fails, the transactions still go in and the sync still succeeds (no checkpoint that time; only the error code is logged). Which types Caisse d'Épargne really sends is unconfirmed: the type is stored in `balance_checkpoints.balance_type` to diagnose.
- **Booking date**: `transactions.booked_on` holds the bank's booking date (synced rows, and the CSV's *Date de comptabilisation*; filled on the kept row when a merge happens). The bank's balance covers what it has booked, so the balance and the gap check count rows by `coalesce(booked_on, date)`.
- **Transactions** (`client.getTransactions`): `GET /accounts/{uid}/transactions?date_from=…` (optionally `transaction_status=BOOK`), following `continuation_key` up to 50 pages (more is an `invalid_response`). When the user started the call, it sends `Psu-Ip-Address` and `Psu-User-Agent` (from `x-forwarded-for` and `user-agent`, `psuFromHeaders`) so the bank counts it as attended. The transaction schema is loose: every field but `transaction_amount` is optional. API amounts (`"12.3"`) become integer cents by string arithmetic, signed by `credit_debit_indicator` (`amount.ts`).
- **Short labels** (`short-label.ts`): the API has no short label, so `stripBankLabel` removes the bank's wrapping (prefixes `CB`, `PRLV`, `PRLV SEPA`, `VIR`, `VIR SEPA`, `VIR INST`; suffixes `FACT ddmmyy`, `DU dd/mm/yy`, a trailing date; `RETRAIT DAB …` and `ECH PRET …` become the bank's fixed `RETRAIT D'ESPECES` and `ECHEANCE DE CREDIT`). That alone gives the CSV's short label for 58 of the sample's 99 rows. `learnShortLabels` maps past CSV rows' stripped long label to the short label the bank showed (most frequent, newest on a tie), so `shortLabelFor` completes truncated names (`BOULANGERIE DES L` → `BOULANGERIE DES LILAS`) and keeps renames (`AUTOROUTE A99 NORD` → `PEAGE AUTOROUTE`).
- **Converting** (`convert.ts`, `convertBankTransactions`), from what a real response showed (SPEC §62): only booked rows (`status` other than `BOOK` is skipped; missing counts as booked). The long label is the remittance lines joined, with the reference the API appends after `FACT ddmmyy` and after the `RETRAIT DAB` id dropped (`cleanBankLabel`), so it equals the CSV's *Libelle operation*; else the counterparty's name, else the transaction code's description. The date is the **operation date**, like a CSV row's: `transaction_date` when sent; else, for a card payment, the date in `FACT ddmmyy` with the year of the booking date (the year before for a December payment booked in January) (`operationDate`); else `booking_date`, else `value_date`. Non-EUR, malformed amounts, no label or no date are row errors. `external_id` is `entry_reference`, set to null when empty, a placeholder (`0`, dashes) or repeated within the response. No bank category (the API has none), so rules and guesses do the categorizing.
- **Start date and window** (`window.ts`): `sync_from` defaults to the day after the account's newest transaction, clamped to [today − 89, today]. The bank is asked from `sync_from` on the first sync, then from the later of `sync_from` and the last sync minus 7 days (late bookings), each minus 4 days of overlap (a card payment made before the start can be booked after it), never before today − 89.
- **Duplicates, one matcher for both ways in** (`match.ts`, pure; `planMatches` and `commitImport` in `src/db/queries/imports.ts` run it for a sync and for a CSV import alike, replacing the Slice 46 overlap rule). It works on the whole batch against the account's existing rows in [first date − 3 days, last date + 3 days], in this order:
  1. **Same `external_id`** (a synced row seen again) is a duplicate.
  2. **Same dedupe hash and occurrence index** (the CSV rule: with the cleaned label and the operation date, most synced rows hash exactly like their CSV row, 277 of 329 on the real check) is a duplicate. Both checks also look at `transaction_matches` (below), so a row merged once never comes back.
  3. **Fuzzy pass** on what is left: same account, **same amount**, date within **±3 days**, and only against the **other side**: a synced row can only match a CSV row that has no bank identity yet (no `external_id`, no sync match); a CSV row can only match a synced row with no CSV match yet. Each candidate absorbs **at most one** incoming row (two €5.88 rows absorb two, not three). Ignored rows are candidates too, so an ignored duplicate stays ignored.
  4. **One candidate** is **merged**: nothing is inserted, the existing row keeps its category, note, tags and splits, and the incoming identity is recorded on it. **Several candidates, exactly one on the same date**: merged with that one (this keeps two coffees on consecutive days out of Review). **Several candidates otherwise**: the row is inserted as a **possible duplicate** and its candidates are stored in `duplicate_candidates`.
  - When a CSV row and a synced row share a hash, the pair is **linked** (`link` on the duplicate decision): the identity is recorded in `transaction_matches` on the existing row, so that row cannot absorb a second purchase later.
  - **Memory**: `transaction_matches` (user, account, kept transaction, the import that brought the duplicate, source, dedupe hash, occurrence index, `external_id`; partial unique indexes on `(account_id, external_id)` and `(account_id, dedupe_hash, occurrence_index)`). Both foreign keys cascade, so undoing the CSV import that made a merge forgets it, and deleting the kept row forgets its matches.
  - Merged and duplicate rows count as duplicates in the result. The unique constraints and `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` stay as the last line of defence (two taps at once insert each row once).
  - **Keep both** is remembered because the matcher only ever looks at **incoming** rows: a row already in franC is never matched again. **Same transaction** moves the row's identity onto the candidate as a match and deletes the row; there is **no Undo** for it (a sync cannot be undone either).
- **Sync now** (`sync.ts`, `syncAccount`, injected client and store; `src/db/queries/bank-sync.ts`): per linked account, fetch, convert, assign dedupe keys, then `commitImport` with `kind: "sync"` (the matcher above, merchant names, rules, guesses, "to review", all in one DB transaction; `transactions.source` = `sync`), then mark `last_synced_at` (and `sync_from` when it was the default). The action (`sync-action.ts`, `syncNowAction`) calls `requireUser()`, checks `BANK_SYNC_EMAILS`, validates the link ids with Zod (the store only finds the user's own active, linked links), passes the PSU headers, and runs `refreshDerivedData` (transfers, recurring) when rows came in. Error codes become sentences; nothing from the bank's response is logged.
- **What is stored**: bank name and country, Enable Banking's session id (useless without the application's private key), consent end date, each bank account's name, currency and **last 4 IBAN characters only**. Never bank credentials.

A Savings account can't be linked to a bank: Bank connection offers only Current accounts (Slice 52). `transaction_source` and `import_kind` gained `franc` for rows franC creates itself.

## Rows added by hand take part in matching (Slice 56)

A waiting hand row (see [transactions.md](transactions.md)) is matched by the same code as the CSV-sync matching, with a new decision, **adopt**. It applies only when the incoming bank row would otherwise be inserted as new, and the hand row is on the same account, still waiting, has the same amount and is within ±3 days (a single candidate, or the one with the same date).

- The hand row is updated in place, so its id, category, note, tags and splits stay. It takes the bank's date, booked-on date, labels, merchant, bank categories, dedupe hash, external id and source, and stops waiting.
- Its own category wins; otherwise the rule or bank category applies.
- No `transaction_matches` row is written: the row now looks like any imported one. Undoing the import leaves it (it belongs to the hidden franC import); re-importing the same file adopts nothing more.
- A hand row is never a candidate for the CSV-sync merge, and hand rows are left out of "newest transaction date", so a lunch dated today does not shorten the first sync's window.
- Adopted rows count as duplicates in the preview and in the sync result.
